👋 Hi, I'm **Jiaxin Fang**, a PhD student at **Fudan University**, where I joined the direct-entry PhD program in 2026. I am currently a **research intern at COCO Matrix**, mentored by [Weikang Wan](https://wkwan7.github.io/), with research guidance from Yuxiang Gao and Mengdi Xu.

My research focuses on **robotics**, especially **world action models, in-context learning, and dexterous manipulation**. I aim to build robot policies that adapt to real-time environmental context and learn from human preferences—without updating model weights. I am also interested in **efficient AI**, accelerating video and image generation through feature caching and reuse, and asynchronous computation. My long-term goal is to make robots more adaptive, capable, and useful in everyday life. **I believe physical intelligence can make the world a better place.**

💬 I'm open to **research collaborations** and always happy to chat about research, studies, and life as an intern. Feel free to reach out!
